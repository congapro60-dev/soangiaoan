Water Cycle Exploration

Grade: 8th | Duration: 45 minutes | Subject: Earth Science

Learning Objectives

  • Students will be able to define the four main stages of the water cycle.
  • Students will demonstrate the process of condensation through a hands-on activity.

Materials Needed

  • Ziploc bags (one per group)
  • Blue food coloring
  • Permanent markers
  • Clear tape

Lesson Procedure

1. Introduction (10 mins)

Begin with a discussion asking students where rain comes from. Introduce the vocabulary terms: Evaporation, Condensation, Precipitation, and Collection.

2. Activity: Water Cycle in a Bag (25 mins)

Have students draw a sun and clouds on the upper half of their Ziploc bag. Add a small amount of water tinted with blue food coloring to the bag. Seal tightly and tape to a sunny window. Observe condensation forming.

3. Conclusion & Assessment (10 mins)

Review the stages observed in the bag. Assign a short exit ticket asking students to describe one stage in their own words.
